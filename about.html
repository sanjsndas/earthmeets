About Earth Meets Architecture

An independent editorial publication exploring the spaces we inhabit — and the materials, light, and ideas that shape them.

Our Mission

Founded in 2021, Earth Meets Architecture exists to demystify the built environment. We publish long-form journalism, practical guides, and critical essays on interior design, architecture, materials, space planning, and sustainable living.

We believe that good design is not a luxury reserved for those with private practices and unlimited budgets. It is a fundamental part of how we live, work, and feel. Our goal is to explain the how and the why behind the spaces around us — without jargon, without pretension, and without hidden agendas.

Editorial independence is non-negotiable. We do not accept sponsored content disguised as editorial. Any commercial partnerships are clearly labelled. Our recommendations are based on research, interviews, and firsthand experience.

What We Cover

Our articles span a deliberately broad range of topics, because the built environment does not fit into neat silos:

  • Interior Design — colour, texture, composition, and the psychology of rooms.
  • Architecture — building typologies, structural innovation, and urbanism.
  • Materials — timber, stone, clay, lime, concrete, and their environmental footprints.
  • Space Planning — human dimensions, circulation, and the geometry of comfort.
  • Lighting — daylight, layered artificial light, and the atmosphere of a home after dark.
  • Furniture — joinery, upholstery, and how to choose pieces that last decades.
  • Sustainable Design — passive principles, embodied carbon, and low-impact building.
  • Small Spaces — micro-apartments, compact homes, and clever storage.

Our Editorial Standards

Every article we publish is researched, written, and edited by humans. We do not use AI to generate editorial content. We cite sources where appropriate, and we correct errors promptly and transparently.

We are self-funded through advertising and reader support. Advertisers have no influence over editorial content. If we ever publish a commercial partnership, it will be clearly marked as such.

Common Questions

Do you accept guest posts?

Rarely, and only from established writers or practitioners. Pitch us first with a clear outline. We do not accept SEO-driven guest posts.

How can I support your work?

Subscribe to our newsletter, share our articles, and consider whitelisting us in your ad blocker. Reader support keeps us independent.

Do you review products?

We occasionally review books and materials, but we do not accept payment for reviews. Any review copies are disclosed.
