Before you write

We receive a high volume of emails. To help us respond faster, please check the following:

  • Pitches: Include a short summary (2–3 sentences) and a link to your portfolio or previous work.
  • Corrections: Please include the article URL and the specific correction needed.
  • Permissions: For republishing, state where and when the content will appear.
  • Student queries: We're happy to help, but please allow extra time during exam periods.

We are unable to respond to every message, but we read everything. Thank you for your understanding.
