Cookies Policy

Last updated: 1 March 2025

This Cookies Policy explains how Earth Meets Architecture ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies (such as web beacons and pixels) on earthmeetsarchitecture.com (the "Site"). It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control our use of them.

Quick summary: We use essential cookies to make the Site work, and optional analytics cookies to understand how readers use our content. You can manage or withdraw consent at any time via our cookie banner or your browser settings.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information to site owners. Cookies set by the website owner (in this case, Earth Meets Architecture) are called "first-party cookies." Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called "third-party cookies."

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use first-party and third-party cookies for several reasons:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the operation of the Site (e.g., remembering your cookie preferences, enabling basic navigation).
  • Analytics and performance cookies: Allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our Site. They help us know which pages are most and least popular.
  • Functionality cookies: Used to recognise you when you return to the Site, enabling us to personalise content and remember your preferences.
  • Third-party cookies: Some articles include embedded content (e.g., videos, charts) from third-party platforms, which may set their own cookies.

4. Your Cookie Choices

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences in the following ways:

  • Cookie consent banner: When you first visit our Site, you will see a banner allowing you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can also change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer.
  • Browser settings: You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our Site, though your access to some functionality and areas may be restricted. The means by which you can refuse cookies through your web browser vary from browser to browser.
  • Opt-out tools: You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that lets you tell websites that you do not want to have your online activities tracked. At this time, our Site does not respond to DNT signals. We continue to monitor developments around DNT and may update this policy if standards change.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised. We encourage you to check this page periodically for any changes. Your continued use of the Site after any modification constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
