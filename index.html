Common Questions

What is the ideal ceiling height?

For living spaces, 2.7–3.0 metres feels generous. Below 2.4 m becomes oppressive. In renovations, removing a suspended ceiling can transform a room.

How much does an interior designer cost?

Fees vary widely: from £50/hr for consultations to 10–15% of project cost for full service. Many designers offer fixed-price room packages.

Is sustainable architecture more expensive?

Often 5–15% upfront, but operational savings and health benefits usually outweigh the premium over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the difference between interior design and interior architecture?

Interior design focuses on finishes, furniture, and decoration within existing walls. Interior architecture involves structural changes — moving walls, altering ceiling heights, and reconfiguring spaces.

How do I start a renovation project?

Begin with a clear brief: how you want to live, not just how it should look. Set a realistic budget (add 20% contingency). Hire an architect or designer early — they save money by avoiding costly mistakes.

What are the most common interior design mistakes?

1. Scale errors (too-small rugs, oversized sofas). 2. Poor lighting (one central fixture). 3. Ignoring circulation. 4. Buying everything at once instead of layering over time.

How can I make a small room feel bigger?

Use low-profile furniture, unify flooring, hang curtains high and wide, choose light colours for large surfaces, and keep sightlines to windows clear. Mirrors help but aren’t magic.
